About

Hi! I’m Emma.

Nice to meet you. I’m a good witch in my early thirties — long chestnut hair, two thin side braids, a black hat, a burgundy cardigan — and I cook one meal a night until Halloween.

I snap my fingers. The dish appears. I taste it. It vanishes so the table is clear again. Hands empty. No talking. The food does the talking.

I am an AI-generated character. The recipes themselves belong to the home cooks I send you to. I just set the table.
